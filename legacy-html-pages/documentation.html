1. Business Passport and Business Identity

Establishing statutory presence, legal records, and corporate identifiers

1 Corporate Legal Entity Registration

Navigate to Business Identity from your sidebar or profile menu. Provide your registered Legal Business Name, Trade Name (DBA), Entity Type (e.g. Private Limited, LLP, Sole Proprietorship), and Date of Incorporation. Enter your 21-character MCA Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or LLPIN.

2 Statutory Indian Tax & MSME Identifiers

Enter your 15-character GSTIN, 10-character Business PAN, and 19-character Udyam Registration Number. In this prototype, identifiers are validated against standard Indian statutory regex rules and persisted securely to your browser storage.

3 Official Contact Channels & Physical Operating Location

Add your official corporate website, verified domain email address, corporate landline phone, and registered office address (Address Lines, City, State, and 6-digit PIN Code).

4 Saving and Applying Changes

Click Save Changes at the top or bottom of the page. All inputs are saved to your account session and instantly synchronized across your Business Passport, Public Profile, and sidebar.

2. Verification Levels (L0–L4)

Understanding the progressive tiered trust model and compliance requirements

The Ohana Trust Protocol structures business credibility into five distinct assurance levels. Higher tiers unlock greater counterparty confidence, simplified vendor onboarding, and preferential terms:

Level 0

Account Claimed

Initial sign-up completed, basic organization profile created without external statutory evidence.

Level 1

Contact Verified

Corporate email domain ownership and mobile telephone two-factor confirmation validated.

Level 2

Business Verified

Statutory registration authenticated: Active GSTIN, MCA Certificate of Incorporation, and Business PAN.

Level 3

Enhanced Verified

Physical commercial operating address confirmed via commercial utility records and banking verification.

Level 4

Enterprise Assured

Comprehensive compliance audit, authorized officer KYC, and cryptographic attestation token issued.

Notice: In this prototype, verification status updates are computed dynamically by the client engine based on completed form fields and submitted evidence files.

3. Uploading and Managing Documents

Organizing statutory certificates, contracts, and tax filings in the Evidence Vault

1 Navigating to the Documents Vault

Click Documents in the sidebar. You will find categorized document tabs including Statutory Registrations, Tax Filings, Licenses & Permits, and Corporate Agreements.

2 Uploading Document Evidence

Click Upload Document to open the upload modal. Select your document type (GST REG-06, PAN Card, Certificate of Incorporation, MSME Certificate, FSSAI, or Shop Act), provide the issuing authority, document reference number, issue date, and expiry date.

3 Document Management and Storage

Uploaded documents are stored within your active user profile partition. You can preview details, filter by category or validity status (Active, Expiring Soon, Expired), and replace certificates when renewed.

↻

4. Tracking Renewals and Email Reminders

Never miss a regulatory deadline with automated reminder schedules

1 Monitoring Active Filing Deadlines

Open the Renewals module to view upcoming renewal schedules for business licenses, certifications, trademarks, and annual compliance returns. Each filing displays days remaining and risk categorization.

2 Automated Reminder Cadence

Ohana triggers automated reminders at 90 days, 60 days, and 30 days prior to license expiry via dashboard alert badges and simulated email dispatches configured in Settings > Email Notifications.

3 Recording Renewal Submissions

When a license is renewed, click Mark Renewed, enter the updated expiration date and upload the latest renewal receipt to reset the compliance clock.

🌐

5. Public Business Profile and Share Room

Showcase verified credentials and securely share due diligence packs

1 Public Profile Visibility Controls

In Public Profile, use the visibility drawer to configure exactly which attributes counterparties can see. You can independently show or hide your GSTIN, official email, telephone, operating address, and MCA CIN.

2 QR Code & Verified Share Links

Click QR Code & Share to generate a shareable URL and smartphone-scannable QR code that allows clients and financial partners to review your verified Business Passport instantly.

3 Download Trust Certificate

Download an official attested compliance summary PDF certificate containing your active trust rating, verification tier, and cryptographic evidence hashes for offline audit submissions.
