Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does L3 Enhanced verification take?

Most statutory checks (GSTIN, MCA, PAN) are validated via automated government portal cross-checks within 15 minutes. Physical address inspections and executive KYC typically conclude within 24 business hours.

How do Share Rooms protect sensitive documents?

Share Rooms generate encrypted, time-limited tokens with optional NDA watermarking and download restrictions. You can revoke access at any time, and every document view is permanently logged.

What happens when a statutory document expires?

Ohana sends automated alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days prior to expiry via Slack, email, and dashboard notifications. Once renewed, simply upload the new certificate to maintain your Trust Score.

Can I assign hardware assets to external contractors?

Yes. Assets can be assigned to full-time staff, contract personnel, or departmental inventory pools with automated tracking of return dates and warranty terms.
